- **Michelle Barker et al. (2022).** Introducing the FAIR Principles for research software. *Scientific Data*, 9(1), 622. [DOI: 10.1038/s41597-022-01710-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01710-x); [PMID: 36241754](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36241754/). - **Roberto Di Cosmo et al. (2026).** CODE beyond FAIR: a roadmap for reusable research software. *Scientific Data*, 13(1), 514. [DOI: 10.1038/s41597-026-06705-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-06705-6); [PMID: 41820417](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41820417/). ## How to read these references

These sources inform documentation, data selection, and model context. The portfolio's numerical results come from its own saved computations and are linked from the relevant repositories. Software practice guidelines do not certify those results. Abstract-only sources are not used to assert detailed methods that were unavailable for inspection. The TOP 2025 guideline article was published in 2026; the CODE publisher's version-of-record date differs from the March date in PubMed. Project-specific references remain in each repository.
