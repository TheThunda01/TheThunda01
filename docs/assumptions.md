# Assumptions behind the portfolio

I want the portfolio to be clear about which choices come from evidence and which make a teaching example manageable. This register covers collection-level claims. Each project has a more detailed register covering its data, biology, statistics, numerical methods, and operating conditions.

| Assumption or choice | Reason and evidence | Check performed | Consequence and remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The examples can develop complementary skills relevant to computational biology. | They address distinct measurements, models, and evidence workflows. This is a learning rationale, not evidence of career outcomes or independent expertise. | The guide identifies each implemented question and its boundary. | Relevance to a particular research group or role remains a judgment for that setting. |
| Small public or synthetic examples are useful starting points. | They are inspectable and economical to rerun. Their size is a practical choice, not evidence that they represent future data. | Input manifests, source identifiers, tests, and worked outputs allow inspection. | Findings remain conditional on these examples. Generalization to new laboratories or populations is untested. |
| Recorded model and data choices are adequate for the narrow demonstrations. | Each project states its own scientific and pragmatic reasons. A published model is a starting point, not automatic validation of a derivative analysis. | Project assumption registers describe sensitivity checks and unchecked conditions. | Wrong or incomplete assumptions can give precise but misleading answers. Numerical consistency alone cannot settle this. |
| A fixed computational record is useful evidence of what was run. | Versioning and documentation support reuse, consistent with [Wilson et al. (2017)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28640806/) and [Di Cosmo et al. (2026)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41820417/). | Source hashes, pinned commits, tests, and saved outputs are compared where implemented. | They do not prove that the original measurements were correct or that no undisclosed decisions occurred. GitHub hosting is not a durable archival guarantee. |
| Related rows are not automatically independent observations. | Shared compounds, donors, plates, sequences, or model assumptions can induce dependence. Grouping addresses a specific overlap risk. | Project reports identify the units they separate and the residual dependencies they cannot test. | Seed variation and grouped partitions do not create independent biological replication. |
| Literature can inform a claim only within the material actually inspected. | A source title or relevant topic does not establish support. Full metadata and access levels make appraisal limits visible. | Citation records were checked against primary sources; relevant accessible passages were read. | Some papers remain abstract-only or metadata-only. Those sources support only the narrower recorded claims. No exhaustive search or independent dual human review is claimed. |
| Documentation consistency helps another reader follow the work. | The shared order and terminology rules are editorial choices, informed by software-practice guidance, not experimentally established usability results. | Mechanical checks cover section order, local links, review-file presence, and audit schema. | They cannot assess all grammar, vocabulary comprehension, scientific validity, or completeness. An independent reader walkthrough remains future work. |
| AI assistance can support implementation and review without demonstrating my personal mastery. | These projects were built and revised with AI assistance. The planned learning checkpoints require an explanation, an independent extension, and analysis of a failure. | The roadmap separates implemented software from learning goals. | Those checkpoints are not claimed complete. Authorship assistance should remain disclosed on a CV and in discussion. |
| A professional pilot requires evidence specific to its setting. | Intended users, inputs, failure consequences, and operational responsibilities vary. The repositories demonstrate methods rather than deployed services. | Practical-use guides identify required checks and acceptance criteria. | Independent use, prospective experiments, clinical usefulness, and autonomous scientific reasoning remain unvalidated. |
